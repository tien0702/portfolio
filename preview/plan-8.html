About

I'm a Unity developer from Ha Noi who loves the small details that make a game feel good — the squish of a tap, the timing of a particle, the haptic that lands just right. My games at Champion Game Studio have reached more than 80 million downloads on Google Play.

I started with C++, Cocos2d-x and SDL, which taught me how memory and rendering really work. Today I lead a Unity team, train junior developers, and maintain a set of in-house packages that every new project starts from.

Outside work I write on my blog and tinker with small games on itch.io.
